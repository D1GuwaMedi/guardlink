Resident

Log in → fill in the visitor's name, phone and the time they may enter → Create. Send the link by SMS (automatic) or press the WhatsApp button. When the visitor registers, the entry code appears in your list.

Visitor

Open the link you were sent, enter your full name and ID number. Show the 6-digit code or QR pass to the guard. Each code works once and only during the valid time.

Guard

Type the code or press Scan QR. Press Check out when the visitor leaves. If the internet fails, keep working: check-ins are saved on the device and sent automatically when the connection returns.

Management

View who is inside, visit history (download as CSV), peak hours, SMS outbox and the audit trail. Add or disable user accounts.
