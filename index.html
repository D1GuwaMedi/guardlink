Safe, simple visitor management

No more paper logbooks at the gate. Residents invite, visitors register online, guards verify in seconds, and management always knows who is inside.

Login How it works

🏠 Residents

Create an invitation for a guest, send the registration link by SMS or WhatsApp, and cancel it any time.

📱 Visitors

Open the link, add your name and ID, and get a 6-digit entry code with a QR pass. No account or app needed.

💂 Guards

Type or scan the code. The system answers Approved or Denied. Works even when the internet drops.

📊 Management

See who is inside right now, full visit history, peak hours, the audit trail, and manage user accounts.

How it works

  1. Resident creates an invitation (visitor name, phone, valid time).
  2. Visitor receives a link and registers with their ID number.
  3. Visitor shows the code or QR pass at the gate.
  4. Guard verifies: the code must exist, be registered, be within time and be unused.
  5. Check-in and check-out are recorded, and the resident is notified.
